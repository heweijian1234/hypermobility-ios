HyperLog — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2024

The short version

HyperLog does not collect any data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking of any kind.

What stays on your device

  • Your joint log entries — dates, severity, notes, triggers
  • Photos you attach (stored inside the app's own storage)
  • Your Beighton self-assessment scores
  • Anything you export or share leaves the app only when you choose to share it

What we never do

  • We never upload, transmit, or sell your data
  • We never access your photos except the ones you explicitly attach
  • We never see, hear, or store anything from your device remotely

Deleting your data

Everything lives on your device. Delete individual entries by swiping them in the app, or delete the app to remove all data permanently.

Feedback

If you use the in-app feedback feature, your message is sent by your own email app, directly to the developer. It contains only what you write (plus, if you made corrections to detected events, a list of those corrections).

Not medical advice

HyperLog is a journal, not a medical device. It does not diagnose any condition. Always consult a healthcare professional.
